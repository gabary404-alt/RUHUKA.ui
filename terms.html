Using the RUHUKA pilot

Pilot use information · Updated 4 November 2026. These are practical rules for the current pilot, not a final school-approved legal agreement. School approval, service ownership and handover arrangements are still outstanding.

Use the right version

The public portal previews are demonstrations. Use only synthetic information there. The separate connected pilot requires an authorised school account and uses database records. The platform has not yet completed school acceptance.

Protect accounts and private information

  • Use your own assigned account. Do not share a password or someone else’s setup code.
  • Open, export or change records only for your authorised school work.
  • Keep private student details out of AI chat and ordinary screenshots sent for support.
  • Sign out on shared devices and report suspected account misuse to the authorised school administrator.

Check important work before submitting

Review attendance totals and the selected class and date. A main register can be submitted only once for that class and day. Ask a Discipline Officer to correct a submitted register with a reason; do not create a second main register to work around an error.

A follow-up lesson is separate from official main attendance totals. Missing records mean “not recorded”; they are not proof of absence or a mark of zero.

People remain responsible for school decisions

Follow-up flags help authorised staff find records that need attention. They do not impose punishments, change official discipline points, or automatically promote or repeat a student. Academic and discipline results remain separate.

AI answers need judgment

The assistant can explain general topics and product workflows. It cannot access private school records, perform school actions, or verify current facts through live web search. Confirm school rules, dates and important decisions with authorised school staff. Do not use a generated answer as an official school instruction.

What still needs agreement

Before handover, the school and project director need to approve the supported devices, pilot results, service costs, data-handling rules, account ownership and support arrangements. Parent messaging, SMS and automatic promotion decisions are outside this first-handover scope.

For a problem with the current pilot, use the steps on Support. Information about storage and service providers is on Privacy.

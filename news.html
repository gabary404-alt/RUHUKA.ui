News & achievements

Stories worth sharing.

Dated achievements and milestones from Lycée de Kigali’s academic, sporting and community life.

From the school

Sport · 26 June 2026

Girls’ basketball: a 3×3 tournament victory

LDK’s girls’ team won the Kenrik & Rosine Memorial Tournament, defeating ESB Kamonyi 12–8 in the final. The tournament brought teams together at Zaria Court in remembrance of Kenrik Kabano and his mother.

Source: Kigali Today · report published 27 June 2026 ↗

Sport · 26 June 2025

A new chapter for volleyball

Rwanda National Police launched the Police Volleyball Academy at Lycée de Kigali. The launch included training equipment for boys’ and girls’ teams, supporting young people’s development through sport.

Source: Rwanda National Police · 27 June 2025 ↗

Heritage · 8 June 2024

Celebrating fifty years

Students, alumni, families and guests came together for Lycée de Kigali’s golden jubilee. The occasion marked fifty years since the school’s establishment in 1974 and celebrated generations of its community.

Source: The New Times · 8 June 2024 ↗

Facilities · February 2023

A renewed home for basketball

The refurbished Lycée de Kigali gymnasium was inaugurated on 10 February 2023 following a project involving NBA Africa and FERWABA. The milestone forms part of the school’s sporting history.

Source: Rwanda Basketball Federation · February 2023 ↗

Academic archive · 14 March 2018

First and third in a mathematics challenge

At the University of Rwanda’s Pi Day competition, LDK student Yvan Gatete placed first and Eric Ishimwe placed third. Students from ten science schools took part in the challenge.

Source: University of Rwanda · 2018 ↗

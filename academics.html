Academics

The school offers secondary education from Senior 1 through Senior 6. Ordinary Level builds a broad foundation before the more focused study of Advanced Level.

Ordinary Level

Senior 1 to Senior 3. Students build foundations in mathematics, sciences, languages and humanities.

Advanced Level

Senior 4 to Senior 6. Subject combinations bring related disciplines together for more focused study.

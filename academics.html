Knowledge with purpose.

From broad foundations to focused study, learning at LDK prepares students for their next step.

Lycée de Kigali offers Ordinary Level and Advanced Level secondary education.

Senior 1–3

Ordinary Level

Build foundations across mathematics, sciences, languages and humanities. This stage develops the understanding and study habits needed for further learning.

Senior 4–6

Advanced Level

Develop more focused knowledge through subject combinations. Contact the school office for the current combinations and guidance for your intended intake.

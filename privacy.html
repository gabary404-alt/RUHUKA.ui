Your information in Lycée de Kigali

Pilot information · Updated 7 November 2026. Lycée de Kigali is still being prepared for school handover. This page describes the current system; the school must agree its final privacy, retention and support arrangements before real school data is imported.

The demonstration and the connected pilot

The public website contains no student records. Its sign-in links open the school workspace pilot, which uses the school database and checks the signed-in account. Older demonstration records can remain on browsers used during development; they are not imported automatically into the school database.

What can be stored

The workspace stores account details, class rosters, attendance, recorded results, announcements and the history of authorised changes. Unsubmitted attendance drafts may remain on the device. Complete result imports and account recovery are still being built.

Who can see it

  • Personal notebooks are postponed. Existing notes have been preserved privately; they have not been deleted or made shared.
  • Online student absence submissions are postponed. Historical private explanations are preserved with their existing access restrictions; they are not public announcements or AI context.
  • Teachers have assigned-class access. Patrons and matrons can take attendance across the school; officer-only corrections and private explanations are separate.
  • Headmaster, Dean and officer access depends on the type of record and their authorised role. Private guardian contacts are kept separate from ordinary lookups and exports.

Before school use, the pilot still needs complete role, account-recovery and device acceptance checks. No access arrangement should be assumed merely because a demonstration screen shows a role.

The AI assistant

When you ask the online assistant a question, your question and recent conversation text are sent through Lycée de Kigali to its AI provider, EjoLabs. The assistant is not connected to student records, private notebooks, absence explanations or account-management tools. It cannot read or change those records.

Do not paste passwords, setup codes, private student information or medical details into chat. The system blocks some obvious credential and record-access requests, but it cannot recognise every kind of sensitive text. Provider retention and processing terms still need review before school handover. The assistant does not have live web lookup.

Hosting and service records

The current website is hosted on Vercel, and the connected database and sign-in use Supabase. These services and the AI provider may process technical request information under their own service arrangements. School ownership, ongoing costs, retention periods and the final privacy notice must be confirmed for handover.

Using a shared phone or computer

Sign out when finished. Do not clear browser data while you need an unsubmitted attendance draft or an older device-only note. Clearing browser data can lose those device-only copies. The active workspace no longer opens personal notebooks.

Correcting information or raising a concern

Contact the school’s authorised administrator through your usual school channel. Explain which record needs attention without sharing passwords. A Discipline Officer handles attendance corrections and absence reviews; the Headmaster manages roster corrections. See Support for practical steps.

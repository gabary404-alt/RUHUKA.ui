Your information in RUHUKA

Pilot information · Updated 4 November 2026. RUHUKA is still being prepared for school handover. This page describes the current system; the school must agree its final privacy, retention and support arrangements before real school data is imported.

The demonstration and the connected pilot

The main website portals contain demonstration records stored in that browser. The separate signed-in pilot uses the school database. They do not automatically share the same records. Do not enter real student information into the demonstration.

What can be stored

The connected pilot can store account details, class rosters, attendance, absence explanations, official results, announcements, personal notes and the history of authorised changes. Some workflows are still being built. Notebook and attendance recovery drafts can also remain on the device you use.

Who can see it

  • Your connected notebook belongs to your signed-in account. It is not a shared staff notebook.
  • Absence explanations go to Discipline Officers. The submitting student can see their own explanation and its decision.
  • Teachers have assigned-class access. Patrons and matrons can take attendance across the school; officer-only corrections and private explanations are separate.
  • Headmaster, Dean and officer access depends on the type of record and their authorised role. Private guardian contacts are kept separate from ordinary lookups and exports.

Before school use, the pilot still needs complete role, account-recovery and device acceptance checks. No access arrangement should be assumed merely because a demonstration screen shows a role.

The AI assistant

When you ask the online assistant a question, your question and recent conversation text are sent through RUHUKA to its AI provider, EjoLabs. The assistant is not connected to student records, private notebooks, absence explanations or account-management tools. It cannot read or change those records.

Do not paste passwords, setup codes, private student information or medical details into chat. The system blocks some obvious credential and record-access requests, but it cannot recognise every kind of sensitive text. Provider retention and processing terms still need review before school handover. The assistant does not have live web lookup.

Hosting and service records

The current website is hosted on Vercel, and the connected database and sign-in use Supabase. These services and the AI provider may process technical request information under their own service arrangements. School ownership, ongoing costs, retention periods and the final privacy notice must be confirmed for handover.

Using a shared phone or computer

Sign out when finished. Before clearing browser data, check that notebook changes say they are saved to the school account; device-only demonstration notes and unsubmitted drafts can be lost when browser data is cleared. Avoid putting private notes into the demonstration on a shared device.

Correcting information or raising a concern

Contact the school’s authorised administrator through your usual school channel. Explain which record needs attention without sharing passwords. A Discipline Officer handles attendance corrections and absence reviews; the Headmaster manages roster corrections. See Support for practical steps.

Our school

A shared history.
A lasting ambition.

Lycée de Kigali is a secondary school in Kigali, Rwanda, with a community spanning generations.

Strive to Excel

Academic learning, practical skills and personal responsibility belong together. LDK encourages students to develop their knowledge, discover their interests and contribute to their community.

Learning is not limited to examination results. Teamwork, curiosity and service also shape the experience of school.

Our history

Five decades. Many generations.

  1. 1974

    A school is established

    Lycée de Kigali was founded with the involvement of the French government.

  2. 1975

    The first students arrive

    The school opened to science students before later welcoming arts students.

  3. 1982

    A new chapter

    The school was handed over to the Rwandan government.

  4. 2024

    The golden jubilee

    On 8 June, the school celebrated fifty years with students, alumni, families and guests.

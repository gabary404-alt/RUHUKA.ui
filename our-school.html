Our school

Lycée de Kigali

Lycée de Kigali is a secondary school in Kigali, Rwanda. The school brings academic study and practical learning together, with an emphasis on teamwork and personal development.

Our motto, Strive to Excel, reflects the school’s focus on learning, effort and growth.

What the school values

Practical learning

Connect what students learn in class to problems they can investigate and solve.

Teamwork

Work with others, share ideas and learn how to contribute to a group.

Personal growth

Build confidence, responsibility and the curiosity to keep learning.

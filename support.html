Help with RUHUKA

This is still a pilot. Use your usual school channel to contact an authorised administrator or the project director. A school-owned support contact will be confirmed before handover. Never send a password or setup code.

First, check which version you are using

Use School sign in from the public page. Your assigned account opens Student, Staff or Admin tools. Attendance submitted here uses shared school records. Older demonstration records are not transferred automatically.

I cannot sign in

Use the account email assigned for the connected pilot. You can request a password-reset email. Email delivery and redirect settings still need acceptance testing. Student-ID login and school setup codes are still being built. If your account has no active access, ask the authorised school administrator to check your role. Do not create a second account to work around the problem.

Attendance says it was already submitted

Open the class submission overview to see who submitted it and when. Ask a Discipline Officer to review and correct the register if necessary. A teacher or patron cannot overwrite someone else’s submitted main register.

The connection dropped while I was taking attendance

Keep the page open. An unsubmitted draft may be saved on that device. Reconnect, refresh the records and check whether someone else already submitted the class. Review the totals before submitting. A device draft is not proof that the school received the register.

A student came late or an absence needs an explanation

Contact the Discipline Office through the usual school channel. An officer can correct the main register to Present, Late or Excused and record the reason. Online student absence forms are postponed; staff attendance does not depend on a student owning a phone. Keep private reasons out of announcements and AI chat.

A student’s name, class or status is wrong

The Headmaster manages roster corrections. A Discipline Officer can submit a correction request in the School desk. Officers can add an individual student; this creates a high priority notification for the Headmaster. Whole-school import permissions and workflows are still being completed.

Where did Notebook go?

Personal notebooks are postponed for the first handover. Existing private notes and older device copies have been preserved. Do not clear browser data if you need an older device-only note; contact the project director about retrieving it.

The AI assistant is unavailable

You can still use local product help. Try again later or ask a school officer about a school-specific issue. The AI does not have private record access or live web lookup. Never share private records to help it answer.

Uploading or correcting academic results

The Dean of Studies opens Academic results, chooses the class, names the release and uploads a CSV. Use student_id first, subject/maximum headers, and overall_percent last. Leave unavailable marks blank; zero means a real mark. Include every class student once. Check the preview, then fix warnings or give a reason before publishing. The Headmaster is notified. To correct a release, open it and choose Correct this release; the older version stays available. School-specific Excel formats are still being connected.

A Discipline Officer needs to import a roster

The Headmaster opens Staff accounts and allows one roster import for the officer. Permission lasts up to 24 hours and ends after one successful import. Invalid files do not use it. The officer opens Roster import and refreshes permission before checking the file. Adding one student does not require this bulk-import permission.

When reporting a problem

  1. Say which screen you used and what you were trying to do.
  2. Include the approximate time, your device and whether the connection dropped.
  3. Copy the visible error message. If sending a screenshot, hide student details, private notes and account information.
  4. Say whether your work is saved, unsubmitted, or showing a conflict warning.

For a disputed attendance, academic or discipline record, ask the responsible school officer to review its recorded source and history. Do not try to fix official data through the AI assistant.

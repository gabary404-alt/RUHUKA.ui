Your school-day guide

These instructions describe the current school workspace preview. Staff can do the daily work without each student owning a phone. Students choose Student at sign-in and use their student ID and password. For first setup or a forgotten password, ask the Headmaster or Discipline Officer for a private one-time code, then open Set up or recover account. Setup codes expire after 24 hours; reset codes after 20 minutes. The student chooses the password. Email invitations and email recovery remain disabled during preview; ask the authorised administrator if you cannot sign in. Never share a password or setup code.

Open your role below. Only the tools allowed for your account appear after sign-in.

Teacher: submit one class register
  1. Sign in with your assigned account. Open Today to see missing attendance.
  2. Open Attendance, check the date and choose your assigned class. Choose Main daily attendance and open the class.
  3. Choose a status for every student. Late counts as attended. Follow the school’s instructions when using Excused.
  4. Review the totals, then confirm. Wait for Submitted to the school. A device draft is not a submitted register.
  5. If someone already submitted, ask a Discipline Officer for a correction. You cannot overwrite their register.
  6. If the roster changed, remaining marks are kept in the draft. Mark new students and review again. A follow-up lesson is a separate register and never changes main attendance totals.

If your assigned class is missing, ask the Headmaster or Dean to check your class access.

Patron or matron: take attendance
  1. Open Attendance and choose the school class and date.
  2. Use the same main-register review and confirmation steps as a teacher.
  3. Check the class submission overview to see which classes are missing and who submitted each register.
  4. After a dropped connection, keep the page open, reconnect and refresh. If another register exists, your draft stays for comparison.
  5. Send corrections through the Discipline Office. Do not put private student explanations in announcements or AI chat.
Discipline Officer: correct and follow up
  1. Open Today and check missing registers, discipline/attendance concerns and notifications.
  2. In Attendance, open a submitted main register. Choose the corrected status, write the reason and save. The previous status and recorder stay in history.
  3. In Discipline records, choose a class, date and upload name. Download the blank totals template. Include each required student exactly once.
  4. Use the school’s official totals, not a deduction calculated in RUHUKA. Add optional dated source events and their original recorder; identify the official source document.
  5. Check the files, compare the preview to the official source, then save. The Headmaster and fellow officers get a notification. No separate approval is required.
  6. To correct an import, open it and choose Correct this import. A totals-only correction keeps earlier events; a new events file replaces them after review. Earlier versions remain available.

You can add one student in School desk; the Headmaster receives a high priority notice. Whole-roster imports need the Headmaster’s one-use permission. Ask for roster corrections through School desk.

Dean of Studies: results and year-end review
  1. Open Academic results, choose the class and name the release. Download the blank CSV template and enter each subject’s maximum.
  2. Keep student_id first and overall_percent last. A blank is Not recorded; zero is a real mark. Include every required student once.
  3. Check the preview. Fix errors, or record why unresolved warnings can be published. Supplied overall percentages determine the saved class ranks.
  4. Publish. The Headmaster is notified. A correction creates a new version and keeps the earlier marks and ranks.
  5. For a manual promote/repeat decision, open School desk → Year-end promote / repeat review. Open one student, choose the review year, results source, decision and configured next-year destination class.
  6. Write your review conclusion, check the preview and explicitly confirm. The default 50% threshold highlights review; it never makes the decision. You can set another yearly review threshold in this tool.

Use Correct this decision for a later reviewed change. Previous decisions and class history remain. The Headmaster manages school-leaver Graduated status separately.

Headmaster: set up and oversee
  1. Use School desk to create classes and set the three term dates. Give teachers their class assignments with the Dean.
  2. Use Staff accounts to review roles and disable access when needed. Email invitations stay unavailable until setup links and delivery have been verified.
  3. Check Today for missing attendance, roster requests, published results, discipline updates and saved class letters.
  4. Review individual student additions and manage name/class/status corrections. Allow a Discipline Officer one roster import when needed; it expires after 24 hours or one successful import.
  5. For year-end work, configure destination classes for the next year before reviewing promote/repeat decisions. A class-year change keeps students in that class; it does not make promotion decisions.
  6. Open Reports for a class/date view or one selected release. A CSV or print view uses the report you loaded. Keep exported student records private.
  7. In School desk → Edit public website text, publish only approved public information. You can authorise named staff editors there. Their permission does not make them a Headmaster.

History starts at the stated baseline. RUHUKA does not invent older enrolment dates or treat missing records as proof that school tasks were completed.

Class letters: Dean, Officer and Headmaster
  1. The Dean or Officer opens Class letters and chooses the class, school year, dates and exact academic/discipline source releases. Missing sources stay Not recorded.
  2. Add an explanation, then Generate letter. Read the fixed school-record summaries and check any numbers you wrote yourself.
  3. Confirm your review and save for the Headmaster. This saves in the school account and creates an in-app notice; it sends no email or SMS.
  4. The Headmaster opens the saved letter. Printing includes the saved date, author and source references. Additional official discipline details may use extra pages.
  5. If source records change later, the earlier letter stays unchanged and shows a notice. An author can prepare, review and save a new version.

Private absence explanations, medical evidence, guardian contacts and notebook text are excluded. Never add them in the free-text explanation. School reporting practice still needs pilot confirmation.

Student: view your own information

When the school supplies a working account, My records shows your own recorded results, attendance and official discipline total. School desk shows notices addressed to you. Missing results say Not recorded. Online absence-submission forms and notebooks are postponed. Ask school staff about a disputed record or access problem.

Named public-page editor
  1. Open School desk → Edit public website text. If it is absent, ask the Headmaster to check your editor permission.
  2. Change the public school sections. Leave unavailable facts blank; do not copy unconfirmed details from an older website.
  3. Confirm school approval, review all public text and then confirm publication.
  4. If another editor published first, copy your changes, reload the current text and compare before trying again. Text is never silently overwritten.

When something goes wrong

  • Connection lost: keep the page open and read its save status. Refresh before retrying a school submission.
  • Already submitted: open the existing record and use the authorised correction route.
  • Could not save a device draft: do not close the page. Copy your marks before reopening or changing devices.
  • Source or roster changed: generate a new preview and review again. Earlier submitted records remain.
  • Cannot sign in: use the assigned account email and contact the authorised administrator. Current recovery status is on the account page.

For help, say which screen and action failed, the approximate time and the visible error. Hide student details in screenshots. Do not send a password. The school-owned support contact is still awaiting confirmation.

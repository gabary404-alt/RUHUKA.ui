Account setup & recovery

Use the email assigned to your school account. A school administrator can help if you do not have access to that email.

Forgot your password?

Check your email and spam folder. Links expire and can only be used once. If an email does not arrive, contact the administrator rather than creating another account.

Email delivery and the school’s approved redirect addresses must be confirmed before handover.

Choose your password

Use at least 12 characters. Avoid your name, student ID or a password you use elsewhere.

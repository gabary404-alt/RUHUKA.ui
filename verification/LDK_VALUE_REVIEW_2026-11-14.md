## Working value proposition

LDK's website gives visitors a clear view of the school and gives authorised staff a shared place to submit attendance, follow up corrections, publish results and prepare reports from recorded information. The intended benefit is less chasing and retyping, with a clear record of who changed what. Actual time, paper and cost savings must be measured in the school pilot.

## Main risk: another place to enter the same information

Karame's publisher lists attendance, marks, enrolment and national-server synchronization; HISP Rwanda describes offline attendance and national data synchronization. These are product claims, not proof that LDK uses all or any of them. Confirm LDK's actual systems, mandatory reporting and exports before replacing a workflow. Do not promise an integration API, remove a mandated record or automate a third-party login without a verified authorised route.

## Feature assessment

| Workflow | Potential practical benefit | What could make it fail | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main attendance and missing-class board | Officer sees who submitted without collecting separate lists | Another mandatory attendance entry remains; staff must scroll too long | Minutes including all duplicate entry; follow-up calls/messages; time officer receives complete register |
| Corrections and recorded history | Fewer disputes about late arrivals and who changed a record | Rules differ from school's real process | Observe a real-shaped correction; verify permission, reason and updated totals |
| Academic imports | Reuse a file rather than retype marks | Preparing our template takes longer than current reporting | Count file preparation, corrections, publication and report-checking time together |
| Discipline snapshots | One traceable official total and source | Officers must recreate existing records just for this platform | Compare complete upload effort with current method; verify who maintains the source |
| Class letters | Reuse existing records in a readable summary | Staff do not prepare or need these summaries | Confirm recipient/frequency and time an actual accepted letter before/after |
| Public school pages | Clearer trusted school information for visitors | Attractive pages alone do not create discovery or enquiries | Record common questions, whether visitors find answers, and attributable enquiries if the school chooses to measure them |
| AI assistant | Quick guidance for common questions | Wrong school facts, unnecessary costs or staff using it instead of simple controls | Common-question accuracy and answer usefulness; no access to private records |

Priorities: reliable accounts, attendance-to-officer flow, imports that fit real files, and usable reports. Keep chat secondary. Do not add fees, payroll, transport, library management, biometric attendance or prediction merely because other school software has them. Add a module only after a specific owner demonstrates an unmet task and a realistic pilot benefit.

## What we still might be solving incorrectly

1. **Typing twice:** establish one authoritative source for each record and a supported exchange path before claiming less work.
2. **File preparation:** a fast upload is not a saving if someone spends an hour reformatting the file. Measure the entire task.
3. **Reports:** the screenshot is not an import specification or approval of annual calculations. Do not label a single-release summary as the official annual report.
4. **Connectivity:** surviving a dropped connection is not full offline operation. Test the school's actual phones and signal; do not claim automatic offline sync.
5. **Decision quality:** a saved notification does not prove a concern was acted on. Agree who checks the board and how follow-up is completed.
6. **Discovery:** a new website does not guarantee national visibility. Current public information, official links, crawlability and useful pages support discovery; impressions and enquiries would need measurement.
7. **Acceptance:** the director can find defects and approve the product direction. Staff must still demonstrate that it fits their daily work without coaching.

## A small, honest measurement plan

Choose the same representative task and comparable class sizes for the old and new methods. Record at least three repetitions when possible, including poor-network conditions. Include preparation, re-entry elsewhere, correcting errors and reporting time; keep setup/training separate and report both. Use role labels, not student names, in the measurement sheet.

Use pilot-measurements.csv. A blank means unmeasured, never zero. Compare medians and ranges rather than picking the fastest attempt. No savings target is claimed as agreed with the school yet.

Monthly staff capacity recovered = (old minutes − new minutes, including remaining duplicate work) × actual task frequency / 60. Report a negative result if the new process takes longer. Time released is not cash saved unless an actual avoidable expense changes. Paper savings require counted sheets/printing costs; subtract service, support and training costs from any monetary claim.

Minimum proposed acceptance: correct totals; no access to another role's private records; no silently lost or duplicated submissions; staff can complete core tasks independently; a demonstrated improvement in an agreed high-frequency workflow; named support and recovery owners. School approval is still required for these criteria.

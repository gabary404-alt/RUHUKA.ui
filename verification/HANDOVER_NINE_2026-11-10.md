# Handover verification, 10 November 2026

The pilot contained no school classes, students or attendance records before this exercise. Disposable synthetic accounts and records were used. The verification preview used server-side fixture authentication and had no real user credentials in its source. Fixtures were scoped to the two named QA classes and five synthetic identities. A fixture-only trigger prevented notices from reaching the existing pilot account.

The race was repeated with temporary scheduled SQL sessions after HTTPS test requests timed out. This proved the overlapping database transactions directly. Each race called the ordinary application RPC with the appropriate authenticated role. The attendance race used a separate class and day from the browser demonstration.

The fixture accounts, sessions, roster, attendance, test jobs, temporary extension and triggers were removed. A post-cleanup count returned one pre-existing account and zero classes, students, registers, temporary triggers or installed pg_cron extension. The temporary Vercel preview credential was replaced with an inert value. The QA endpoints and fixture code are absent from the release branch.

169 Node tests passed. The four affected rollback suites also passed. The first-handover tracker records 86 verified, 30 open and 12 postponed. The physical device matrix, live school acceptance, and email delivery remain open.

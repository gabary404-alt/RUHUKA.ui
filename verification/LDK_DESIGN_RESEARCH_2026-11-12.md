# Lycée de Kigali visitor website research and design brief
Date: 12 November 2026 (Africa/Kigali)
Status: research and visual choices for director review; not a deployed redesign.

## Decision
Make the public website unmistakably Lycée de Kigali. RUHUKA is the developer company, not the school or chatbot. Use “LDK’s AI assistant” in the launcher, heading, accessible labels, greeting and server instructions. Developer credit may remain discreet in the footer if agreed. Keep technical identifiers stable unless a separate migration is justified. No new school facts from this report are approved for publication yet.

## Proposed public structure
Home; Our School (history, approved values, leadership welcome); Academics (confirmed levels/combinations and learning); School Life (confirmed clubs, sport, support); News & Achievements (dated evidence); Admissions & Visits (only the school’s confirmed process); Contact. A secondary School sign in remains for members. Recommend normal same-tab internal navigation, with homepage section links where appropriate; this changes the earlier explicit new-tab preference and needs director agreement.

Use warm ivory, deep school green, charcoal body text and restrained brass accents as a proposal, not an asserted official palette. Refined serif headings with a simple readable body face; flatter editorial sections, fewer decorative cards/pills, no glow, stars or cute mascot on the institutional hero. A discreet text/chat icon can remain. Use one hero image and selective supporting art, not all ten mood studies. Reserve image dimensions, responsive compressed assets, lazy-load below-fold art and no autoplay video/canvas. Set and measure a performance budget during implementation.

## Existing website findings
Source HTML includes numerous empty href links and navigation pointing to 404 pages. About photos have malformed trailing slash paths which returned 404 in retrieval. The academics menu includes “Our Doctor”; about content includes a medical-template phrase. Homepage testimonials repeat unnamed “Students Name” placeholders. Contact page has a malformed phone variant. Search-index counts differ from fetched counters; no current student/staff/club count is reliable enough to republish. Staff names/personal phones and “this year” claims need school confirmation. Third-party social image URLs can expire. These are observed defects, not proof every form/backend is broken; no messages or forms were submitted.

An additional official-domain subsite https://www.one.lyceedekigali.ac.rw/index.php has old 2024 announcements and an inconsistent email spelling. Do not reuse its admissions dates.

The supplied Google short link resolved in the connected browser to https://ldkedu.com/ and displayed a learning dashboard. This differs from the older official-domain site. Do not infer ownership, live functionality, or school approval from branding. Ask director which site is intended as the current public reference. Do not copy any account-specific data from the dashboard. This research does not claim a 100% private-feature audit.

## Launch acceptance additions
- School identity across header, title, metadata, chatbot UI/system prompt, accessibility labels, workspace, reports and emails. Developer company only in agreed credit/legal contexts.
- Remove developer-only prose and demo credentials from production routes and bundles; verify demo entry routes are actually excluded. Keep test-environment notices in test environments.
- Remove “pilot” and “unfinished” text only after the underlying limitation is resolved or feature withheld. Retain useful user-facing saving/offline/error/privacy disclosures where they affect real choices.
- Recheck public navigation, contact details, approved content and image/logo permissions.
- Re-run mobile, keyboard, contrast, reduced-motion and loading checks after redesign.
- No new application workflow or original checklist ID was completed by research. Existing counts remain historical technical evidence, not redesigned-site acceptance.

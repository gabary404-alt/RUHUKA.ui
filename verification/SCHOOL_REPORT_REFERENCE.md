## Student print and discipline format

The director confirmed that the school has no standard discipline upload file. RUHUKA provides its own blank CSV templates. Enter a school-confirmed total for each student in the class. The total is out of 40 and is taken directly from the official school record. The optional events file includes student ID, unique event key, date, reason, original recorder and reported points change. If a change is unknown, leave it blank. Enter IDs as text so that 001 stays 001.

The student sees their own official total with its snapshot date, and a separate list of dated events. For example, a confirmed total might be 32 / 40, with a separately recorded change of −2 for one event. The event list does not change the official total. A confirmed zero is displayed as zero. Missing totals, changes and recorders say Not recorded. Results and discipline remain separate.

The academic printout uses the school heading, motto, student/class/year, a subject table, supplied overall and published position. It prints one saved release and omits internal staff review notes. The screenshot is a visual example. The school’s actual import columns, annual weights and grading rules still require confirmation before making an official annual report.

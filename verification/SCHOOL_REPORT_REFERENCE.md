# School report reference — 7 November 2026

Director supplied a phone screenshot of a student annual report. Used only as a visual reference; no student name/identifier, marks, QR data, contact details, signature or raw image is committed to this repository.

Reliably visible structure: school identity above a STUDENT ANNUAL REPORT title; student/class/year fields; subject rows grouped by three terms and an annual section; totals/percentage/position; grading key and Headteacher comments/decision area. The small subheadings, precise maxima, complete grading boundaries and annual calculation method cannot be reliably read at the supplied resolution. Do not infer or hardcode them.

This phase adds a school-branded one-release student results summary with subject maximum/obtained/percentage, supplied overall, separately calculated weighted total, saved rank, release/date/version and missing/zero distinctions. It is clearly labelled as not the official annual report. It uses authorised existing data and adds no signature, logo scan, QR verification or fabricated approval.

F02 remains open for a legible original PDF or school marks spreadsheet and confirmed mapping. C07 remains open because roster and discipline source examples are still missing. A screenshot of one student's report is not a class import file. Annual aggregation, grading bands and official report authenticity must be confirmed before offering that report as school-approved output.
